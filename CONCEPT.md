# CONCEPT
In Swordverse, **The Orders** are the core concepts that define the structure and functionality of the game. They represent the fundamental principles that guide players in their journey through the Swordverse universe. Each Order encompasses a unique set of techniques, actions, and strategies that players can utilize to achieve their objectives.

Currently, there are 5 ORDERS that rule the Swordverse:
### True Sword Order
**Description:** The True Sword Order is the pinnacle of sword mastery, representing the ultimate path for those who seek to wield the sword with unparalleled skill and precision. Members of this Order are known for their unwavering dedication to the art of swordsmanship and their ability to harness the true essence of the sword.

**Playstyle:** Balanced swordsmanship, mastering every aspect of combat.
>Balanced • Sustain • Resilient • Origin

### Divine Sword Order
**Desciription:** The Divine Sword Order is a sacred and revered Order that embodies the divine power of the sword. Members of this Order are often seen as protectors of the realm, wielding their swords with a sense of righteousness and purpose. They are known for their ability to channel divine energy through their blades, making them formidable opponents in battle.

**Playstyle:** Overwhelming righteous power that grows stronger as battle continues.
>Scaling • Heavy • Fragile • Mythical

### Demonic Sword Order
**Description:** The Demonic Sword Order is a dark and mysterious Order that draws its power from the forces of darkness. Members of this Order are often feared and misunderstood, as they harness the destructive potential of their swords to achieve their goals. They are known for their cunning and ruthless tactics, making them a force to be reckoned with in the Swordverse.

**Playstyle:** Explosive destructive power at the cost of control and stability.
>Aggressive • Burst • Risky • Mythical

### Flying Sword Order
**Description:** The Flying Sword Order is a unique and agile Order that specializes in the art of aerial combat. Members of this Order are known for their ability to wield their swords with incredible speed and precision, often striking from above with devastating effect. They are masters of mobility and can outmaneuver their opponents with ease.

**Playstyle:** Relentless offense through speed, deception, and unpredictable strikes.
>Swift • Combo • Adaptive • Spirit

### Shadow Sword Order
**Description:** The Shadow Sword Order is a mysterious and elusive Order that thrives in the shadows. Members of this Order are known for their ability to manipulate darkness and strike from unexpected angles, making them formidable adversaries. They excel in stealth and deception, often using their surroundings to their advantage in combat.

**Playstyle:** Precise and calculated strikes, utilizing stealth and misdirection to outwit opponents.
>Technical • Combo • Evasive • Mastery
